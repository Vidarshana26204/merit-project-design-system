Quilt Map · icosphere tree vs low-poly tree (live)

EnergyPick one

Two takes on the picked tree, both live on a snapshot of GET /public/explore (A/L Science: 6 subjects; Combined Mathematics has no topics yet, so it is muted). A (picked) is the icosphere tree on a hill: chunky low-poly crowns and the fractal recursion you liked, three levels deep (subject, then its topics, then that topic's subtopics), each level blooming out of the crown you opened. B is the round 12a faceted tree, kept for reference.

A. Spec. Scene. Projected 3D in SVG (no three.js) + GSAP. Design space 640 x 600 (16:9 stage: 1067 x 600 with the tree shifted right 427). The tree fills the frame: crown top at about 8% of the height, trunk base (410, 538), fork (352, 420); the trunk leans left towards the copy, forks into 3 to 4 angular branches, and stands on a grass hill. No card: the sky is the page background. Projector. Vertices are rotated by yaw then pitch about the crown pivot (330, 250) and perspective-projected (focal length 900). Back faces are culled (face normal against the vector to the camera); faces are painter-sorted by view depth, and so are whole objects (bark gets a 40 unit bias behind balls). Shading. Lambert against a key light from the top left, view-space (-0.5, -0.65, 0.58), quantised to 5 tones: lit < 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, then brighter. Tone mixes of the ball's base colour: 30% black, 16% black, base, 20% white, 38% white (dark theme: 15% black, base, 12%, 26%, 44% white). Crown (12c). Two kinds of icosphere (subdivision 0, about 10 visible faces, vertex radius jittered 0.9 to 1.1). Node crowns: at most 6, small (r 56 to 62; "+N more" r 40), vivid, z 22 to 46 so they always sit in front, in a wide fan at (330,92) (178,142) (486,136) (92,238) (546,236) (262,236), filled centre-out. Neutral background crowns: 8 to 12, not nodes (aria-hidden, no pointer events), bigger (r 54 to 116), further back (z -70 to -120), desaturated sage-olive --qm-bg-leaf-1..3 (light #7F9A79 #94AA87 #AAB99A, dark #3D5240 #4A6049 #586B52) at 3 compressed tones, bobbing half as far and slower. They make the canopy one mass so the vivid nodes read as the things to touch. Palette (dedicated vars --qm-leaf-1..5, not the grey sage tokens): #1F7A4D #2FA35C #49C26B #8FD86A #C9E36B, one per ball by hash; dark theme #176339 #228247 #37A056 #6EB454 #A5BC55 (deeper, less luminous). Bark --qm-bark #7A4A2A with shadow faces --qm-bark-dark #4E2E1A, 5 tones. Grass gradient #8FD86A to #2FA35C (dark #5FA83D to #1D7A47). The hover tint to the subject colour is kept (0.35 s transition on the base colour, tones re-derived live). Branches (12c). Tapered hexagonal prisms with a kink. Trunk 28 to 15 wide; three boughs leave the fork to (226,300) (346,236) (470,286), 14 to 8 wide; every node crown has its own twig (7.5 to 3.8 wide) from the nearest bough to just under it, so the structure reads at a glance. Hill. A grass mound over the right 60% of the stage, crest 16% of the height, with 14 low-poly tufts in front of the trunk base. Items. At most 6 interactive balls per level: subjects in data order, topics ranked by exam share (x) descending; with more, the "+N more" ball opens the full list in the panel (production: the Subjects page). Labels (12d). Never trimmed: always the full name, no short forms, no ellipsis. Wrapped into balanced lines (1 line up to 14 characters, 2 up to 28, else 3), white bold 15 px on screen with a soft dark drop shadow, centred on the crown's lower face; when the widest line is wider than 1.9 r the font scales down to fit (never below 0.55x). Labels live in one top layer and are nudged apart. On hover or focus a surface pill with a focus ring appears under the text; "+N more" keeps a small pill. Interaction. A ball is <g role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="Physics, 11 topics">. Recursion (12c). The trunk is the stream. The opened crown is the carried object: its facets become the child crowns, and the branch that held it stays solid, so the new tree visibly grows out of the branch you touched (its fork sits on that twig's tip, scale 0.56). A topic blooms the same way into its subtopics (top 6 + "+N more"); a subtopic is the last level: picking one squashes it, pulses it to 1.06 and dims the rest, and the topic panel stays open. Going up several levels at once (breadcrumb) runs the outer collapses at 2x. Motion. Grow on load, about 1.8 s (power2.out): the hill fades in 0 to 0.45 s, the trunk extrudes (scaleY from the base) 0.25 to 0.8 s, branches extend from the fork at 0.65 s (70 ms apart), balls pop with back.out(1.8) from 0.95 s, 60 ms apart ordered by distance from the fork, labels fade in. Idle: balls bob +/-2 units, 2.2 to 3.6 s, phase from the hash; every ~8 s a gust spins each ball up to 0.2 rad, staggered left to right (a ripple across the crown). Parallax: the whole scene yaws +/-6 degrees and pitches +/-3 degrees following the pointer, eased 0.9 s. One render loop, re-projecting only when something changed. Transition motion (12c, from the motionmaxxing measured references; frames at 30 fps). Eases: softLand cubic-bezier(.22,.9,.3,1), snapSettle (.12,.95,.2,1), glide (.33,.75,.4,1), gentleIn (.55,0,.85,.45), accelExit (.6,0,.9,.35). Landings decelerate, exits accelerate, opacity is only a 4 to 6 f helper, one spring per transition. Drill-in, about 1.2 s: (1) press: crown squashes to 0.94 in 2 f, answers at 3 f (to 1.02, softLand 5 f). (2) siblings leave nearest-first on gaps 2,1,3,2,2 f: drift 40 units outward, scale 0.7, accelExit 12 f, opacity off over the last 5 f; neutral crowns half the drift, 15 f. (3) camera: one viewBox move from 3 f, snapSettle 20 f (fast front, long tail) to the child view; the parent's other wood is swept to 0.05 (gentleIn 12 f) inside it. (4) bloom from 8 f: boughs grow from the fork (softLand 10 f, 2 f apart); each twig grows (softLand 12 f) on offsets 0,2,4,7,8,10,12 f from 12 f while its share of the opened crown's facets travels the bough (gentleIn 5 f) then the twig (softLand 5 f), shrinking to 0.4; each child crown lands 10 f later from 1.25x (snapSettle 11 f, 4 f opacity helper); the last one is the spring (to 0.92 in 8 f, then back.out to 1 in 6 f); labels follow 3 f after their crown (6 units down, softLand 10 f); neutral crowns fill in behind last. (5) breadcrumb hard-cuts on; idle bob resumes. Drill-out, about 0.9 s (a designed reverse, not a rewind): child labels hard-cut off; child crowns leave farthest-first (to 0.4, gentleIn 7 f, gaps 1,2,1,2 f) while their facets fly home (softLand 10 f) and reassemble the crown; twigs then boughs retract (accelExit 8 f); camera pulls back in two pushes (gentleIn 6 f to 30%, softLand 14 f home); siblings and neutral crowns return nearest-first from 0.85 (softLand 12 f). Topic: squash 2 f, pulse to 1.06 (glide 4 f) and back (glide 6 f); the rest dims to 0.45 (glide 8 f). Hover: lift 4 units and 1.04 (glide 6 f in, 8 f out). A new request while a transition runs makes it finish at 2x first. Review hook: QMIco.debug(i).drill(name), then .seek(0..1). Reduced motion: finished and still: no parallax, no bob or gust; the camera and the recursion jump.

B. Spec. Build. 2.5D SVG + GSAP; every facet is a real <polygon>. Stage viewBox 800 x 700, trunk base (400, 598); the home camera is fitted to the real crown facet bounds, every cluster and the ground, +24 units padding and a 6 unit sway margin (plus room for the chips in the interactive mount), so the tree fills its column (about 560 x 490 in the hero). Layout is deterministic (FNV-1a hash of node ids; grow() ported from the picked tree): trunk 140 units, branches 130 x 0.82 per level, tips relaxed to at least 0.78 x cluster diameter apart (so clusters overlap). Trunk and roots. 44 wide at the base tapering to 15 at the first fork, plus 4 short root triangles spreading 26 to 50 units along the ground. Branches. 15 x 0.68depth-1 wide (min 2.4), each a strip of 5 steps x 2 triangles with an S-bend of up to 6% of its length, plus 2 to 3 decorative twigs (1 on thin ones), thin triangles 11 to 22 long. Bark is warm brown, orange-700 50% / ink-700 then 74% over sand-300, in 4 tones by facing (black 22% to white 34%). Twigs and bark are aria-hidden. Crown. One decorative foliage hull behind the bark, a domed cloud: bottom 0.9 R below the lowest tip, half-width = spread/2 + 0.5 R (the rim runs through the outer clusters), half-height at least 0.62 x half-width (it grows upward, never below the bottom). The outline has 4 and 7 lobe bulges (+/-9% and 5%) and an underside that rises about 34% towards the trunk, so sky shows between the crown and the branches; the bounds are about 1.9:1. 30 rim + 20 + 10 inner points + a crown point, triangulated with Delaunator into about 100 facets; aria-hidden, no pointer events. Sage ramp (sage-500 62% / sage-700), same six-tone light model but lower contrast (82% black to 75% white); dark theme uses sage-700 / sage-900 lifted towards sage-500. Subject clusters sit in the crown and overlap its edge, as subject-coloured patches (the quilt nod): radius R = 1.3 x clamp(72 - 2.2n, 27, 62). Points: 12 outer (0.8 to 1.14 R, 1.12 wide), 8 mid ring (0.4 to 0.6 R) and a crown point, dome height up to 0.98 R; Delaunator gives about 28 to 32 facets. Shading. Facet normal from the 3D triangle dotted with a light from the top left (-0.55, -0.65, 0.5), plus +/-0.08 jitter, quantised to six tones at -0.05, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8. Subject cluster colour: subject 76% black darkest, the subject colour in the middle, subject 52% white lightest (dark theme lifts the shadows: 90% black to 46% white). A subject with no topics uses 20% of its colour over --merit-text-tertiary, saturate(.5), and "coming soon" in its aria-label. Labels. Never clipped: long titles use short forms (ICT, Agri Science, Combined Maths, Media Studies...), then wrap onto as many lines as needed (11 characters per line for subjects, 12 for topics). All pills are drawn in one top layer after every cluster (also at topic level), so no cluster covers a label; overlapping pills are nudged down until clear. Pills (surface, 1.2 border, 17.5 px bold) sit just below the cluster centre toward its lower edge, and scale with the camera zoom so they stay about 12 to 14 px on screen. Streams with more than 12 subjects (Arts) show the label on hover or focus only. Grow on load, about 1.8 s (one GSAP timeline, power2.out): trunk scaleY 0 to 1 from the base, 0 to 0.5 s; branches scale 0 to 1 from their start point, 0.34 s each, depth d starting at 0.3 + 0.1 d; crown facets pop (back.out(1.7), 0.3 s, 6 ms stagger, centre outward) from 0.8 s; then each subject cluster pops its facets (8 ms stagger) from 1.3 to 1.4 s by hash, its label fading in 0.3 s later. Idle. Each cluster sways +/-1 degree about a point 0.9 R below its centre, sine.inOut, 3 to 5.2 s per half cycle, phase from the hash of its id. The coin tour: every ~6 s a butter coin travels the branches to the Physics tip over 1.7 s (sine.inOut), the cluster bumps to 1.07, and "+10" rises 26 units and fades in 1 s; it stops for good at the first pointer, key or focus event. Interaction. Each cluster is <g role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="Physics, 11 topics">; click or Enter/Space opens it. The camera (viewBox) tweens 0.8 s power3.inOut to a box fitting the new sub-tree; the crown, trunk, other subjects and the home branches fade out (the crown to 0, the rest to 10 to 18%, and they leave the tab order), the subject cluster shrinks to 0.4 and fades (0.45 s) while its topic sub-branches (bark coloured, with twigs; only the tip clusters carry the subject tint) grow out of the tip (0.3 s each) and one smaller cluster per topic (R = 36 - 0.7n, 21 to 31) pops in, 30 ms apart. Clicking a topic dims the others to 30%, rings the chosen one and fills the panel: counts, exam share, CTA "Start practising X" to sign-up with the subject. Esc or the breadcrumb reverses it (the crown grows back in place). Reduced motion: final state instantly, no sway, no coin, camera jumps. Lenis is production smooth scroll only; it is not used here.

3. Mesophyll

The mesophyll has two layers. Palisade cells sit under the upper epidermis. They are long, tightly packed and full of chloroplasts.

Spongy cells below them are loosely arranged, with large air spaces for gas exchange.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Exam tip. The 2019, 2021 and 2023 papers asked you to label palisade and spongy mesophyll on a T.S.
