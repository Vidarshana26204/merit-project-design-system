Phase A specimen. It shows the proposed palette, display-face candidates, motifs and the core components, built on the warm-canvas, orange and violet grammar of the reference. Every value here is a proposal for the checkpoint and is not shipped.

01 · Palette

Warm energy, violet depth.

These hue families were sampled from the reference: orange to coral to sand for energy, violet for focus and authority, and sage as the one cool accent. Butter (rewards) and sky/blush (subject categories) were added to cover Merit's own needs.

02 · Decision D9

The orange problem.

White on brand orange fails AA for button text: the reference #FC661E is 2.98:1, and the tuned v3 #F65E16 is 3.22:1, which passes only for large text. There are two ways to keep orange as the primary action.

A · recommended

Vivid orange #F65E16 fill with an ink label (5.22:1); the fill clears 3:1 on canvas. This keeps the reference's energy.

B

Burnt-orange fill #B23F06 with a white label (5.82:1). It is safer for long labels but reads brown and duller.

03 · Decision D8

Display face candidates.

Each card renders Merit content in its candidate face. Body text stays Plus Jakarta Sans, and SI/TA fall back to Noto. Use the picker at the top to apply a face to the whole page.

04 · Illustration kit

Patchwork subjects.

Each tile is a rounded square cut into geometric patches, each filled with a tint or pattern (dots, stripes, checks), all outlined in ink. A Phosphor Duotone glyph sits on the white focal patch. Sparkles and dotted connectors are the motifs.

05 · Components

Core pieces, both modes.

Calm mode, used for quiz-taking and notes: canvas, slate text and violet selection. Energy mode, used for home, rewards and landing: orange, butter and sparkles.
