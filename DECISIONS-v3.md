# Merit DS v3 decisions

These decisions continue the v2 log (D1–D7, SKILL.md §9).
- **Status:** ✅ approved · ❌ rejected. D8–D15 were approved as recommended on 2026-10-16.
- **Evidence:** `research/v3/*`, plus the live specimen `research/v3/specimen.html`

## Phase A checkpoint (approved as recommended)

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| D8 | **Display face is General Sans** (500/600/700 + italics), self-hosted. Body stays **Plus Jakarta Sans**. SI/TA fall back to Noto Sans Sinhala/Tamil as before. Alternative: Satoshi, which can also supply a Black Italic wordmark voice. | Scored 90/100 (see `display-face-evaluation.md`). It has a true italic for the emphasis word, a geometric voice that matches the reference, and the same ITF FFL licence as today's Clash Display. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D9 | **Primary action is orange `#F65E16` with an ink label** (`#1E1B2E`, 5.22:1). White-on-orange is never used for text. A strong variant, burnt orange `#B23F06` with white (5.82:1), exists for dense or long-label contexts. | The reference orange `#FC661E` fails with white (2.98:1) and against the canvas (2.85:1). Shifting it to `#F65E16` keeps it visually identical while clearing 3:1 on the canvas. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D10 | **Palette families:** a warm **canvas** (`#FBFAF4`); **ink-slate** text (`#1E1B2E` / `#4A4760` / `#68667F`); **orange to coral to sand** for energy; **violet** for depth, focus, selection, links and the Achiever tier (`#54306C` / `#663696`, with electric `#7E24DE` for sparkles only); **sage** for success; **butter** for rewards and coins; **blush/sky** for categories only; **red** for error. | Sampled from the reference and extended for gamification (butter) and subject categories. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D11 | **Depth model:** no hard offset shadows. Soft elevation `e0`–`e3` (warm violet-tinted, at most 2 layers, blur ≤ 40). **Cards have no border.** Ink outlines (1–1.5 px) appear only on illustrative elements, filter chips and patchwork. Card radius is 24 and pills stay. | This matches the reference's flat and airy look. It drops the v2 signature, and that is the intended break. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D12 | **Icons:** Phosphor **Regular** by default (it was Bold), **Fill** for active and selected states, **Duotone** on category and feature chips. The streak icon is `flame` (Fill, orange) and the points icon is `coins` (Fill, butter). | Regular's stroke weight matches the 1–1.5 px outline grammar, and Duotone echoes the patchwork. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D13 | **Landing font:** School Times is retired. The landing uses the D8 display face, and the wordmark voice uses a heavy italic (General Sans Bold Italic or Satoshi Black Italic). | One display family across product and marketing, in the style of the reference's italic wordmark. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D14 | *(Superseded for subject tiles by D16.)* **Illustration kit "patchwork":** rounded-square tiles cut into 3–5 patches, filled with a tint or a pattern (dots, stripes, checks, hatch), with ink outlines, a white focal patch and a Duotone glyph. The motifs are a 4-point sparkle, a brush underline and a dotted connector. Patterns are SVG and never raster. | It makes subjects instantly recognisable and powers the new **learning path map** (the hierarchy shown as connected tiles). | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D15 | **Modes:** *calm* (quiz-taking, notes: no sparkles, violet selection, at most one orange action) and *energy* (home, rewards, landing). **Dark mode** uses a violet-black base (`#17121F` / `#221B2C`) with orange lifted to `#FF8A4C` and violet links at `#C9B3EA`. **Compact density** for admin is carried over from v2 D7. | See `edu-theming-criteria.md` C2, C5 and C8. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
| D16 rev 3 | **Patchworks v3.2 (engine v3):** no ink seams: neighbouring tints blend through a gradient strip along each ray and every pattern dissolves near seams (patterns become scalable elements). The `corner` variant is replaced by `wash`: the subtopic's added pattern fading from a card's top-right corner, `currentColor`, tinted per theme from manifest inks. Icon picks get a report-only Jev audit; a person approves swaps. | Hard seams read busy next to dense patterns; a whole patch body in the card corner fought the copy, while the one pattern a subtopic added is its most specific mark and stays quiet behind text. | ✅ 2026-10-17 |
| D16 rev 5 | **Patchworks v3.4 (engine v5): subject colour.** The subject's `color_hex` (backend `subjects.color_hex`, mirrored in merit-content `subject.json`) sets an OKLCH palette for the subject and every node below it: pastel tints around its hue plus a soft complement, the colour itself as the solid patch, and icon accents. The colour is part of the signature. Plate sides equal the patch count: square subject, pentagon topic, hexagon subtopic, heptagon quest. | Hand-picked families ignored the subject colour the app already shows, so chips and progress bars disagreed with the art. One hue per branch makes a node's subject legible at a glance, and sides = patches makes the level countable. | ✅ 2026-10-18 |
| D16 rev 4 | **Patchworks v3.3 (engine v4): square in a square.** Fixed cuts symmetric on both axes and both diagonals replace jittered radial wedges: subject = hourglass X + corner triangles, topic + diamond, subtopic + square over the diamond (quest + a band). The plate's shape says the level: hexagon, pentagon, square; plates and icon (136/256) are larger. Seams are crisp tint edges, patterns still dissolve. The 4:1 `row` variant is retired; the tile is used everywhere. App-feature pack icons are deferred (Phosphor). | The radial wedges had no axis of symmetry and read as random; nesting makes the hierarchy literal (a topic visibly contains its subject's block) and the plate shape names the level at a glance. One tile size-scales cleanly to rows, so the row variant only doubled output and maintenance. | ✅ 2026-10-18 |
| D18 | **Patchwork mastery fill:** a tile showing progress may be drawn as a 35% greyscale base plus the colour tile clipped to the mastery %. The only sanctioned alteration of a tile (SKILL §12). | The Learn quilt reads as a quilt being stitched; a ring around an untouched tile duplicates the hub's ring. Scoped to progress so tiles stay recognisable everywhere else. | ✅ 2026-10-21 |
| D19 | **Colour-only timer:** digits + icon recolour (`timer.text / warnText / criticalText`); no pill. Pill tokens deprecated, removed in v4. | Wireframe review: the butter/red pill was too distracting in calm mode. Butter fails as text on light, so light warns in orange-700. | ✅ 2026-10-21 |
| D20 | **Subject hub tint:** `mix(color_hex, subject.headerBase)` at 0.30 light / 0.24 dark (+ 0.40 rim in dark). Text limited to primary/secondary. | Checked against all 62 subject colours: the wireframe's 58% failed secondary (3.26) and eyebrow (2.13) on mid-tone subjects like Economics; 0.30/0.24 pass every subject in both themes. | ✅ 2026-10-21 |
| D17 | **Feature banners:** a violet stadium with hairline arcs and a clipped orange disc (reference: user screenshot 2026-10-17). Band and disc each carry a reserved pattern pair per context that dissolves toward the copy. | The v3.0 band repeated one colour + shape everywhere; per-context pattern pairs keep banners related but distinct, and dissolves keep the copy legible. | ✅ 2026-10-17 |
| D16 | **Hierarchy patchworks** (v3.1): every Subject, Topic and Subtopic gets a generated patchwork (pack icon centred on a white plate, radial wedges of tints and 16 geometric patterns all meeting at the icon, no sparkles; 4 patches for a subject, then each child keeps its parent's patches and adds one: topic 5, subtopic 6). Subject icons are unique globally, topic and subtopic icons unique per subject, and compositions are unique across all nodes, keyed by `sha256(node_id ‖ icon_sha256 ‖ engine)` plus a salt registry. Variants are tile, row, corner and mono. Patchworks are reserved for hierarchy widgets, and state spots become line art. The licensed pack and output live in private merit-content, and the backend serves URLs via `hierarchy_patchworks`. | Six shared category tiles could not tell 296 nodes apart, and they blurred with empty states. Generated, checksum-signed compositions give each node a stable identity, scale to new subjects and Quests, and keep the licensed art out of this public repo. | ✅ 2026-10-16 |
