Mobile number

Sri Lankan mobile numbers are +94 7X XXX XXXX: a 7, then 8 more digits. Today the field accepts any first digit. Applies to Register, Sign in (Mobile tab) and Forgot password.

The student can't type a wrong first digit at all: the 7 is printed in the prefix, the keyboard only fills the 8 digits after it. Pasting "0769857985" or "+94769857985" is cleaned to the 8 digits.

Students who habitually type the leading 0 or 7 need the paste cleaner; typing 7 first is ignored.
